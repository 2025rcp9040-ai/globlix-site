Globlix exists because exporting from India is harder than it should be. Good products stay in the domestic market because the first steps (finding a buyer abroad, getting the licences right, booking freight, getting paid and claiming benefits) are spread across too many offices and portals.

## Built by practitioners

Globlix is backed by **QOPTUM Private Limited**, which has run its own export business since 2017. The problems we solve for clients are ones we have solved for our own shipments.

## How we work

- **One window.** One team for research, compliance, logistics, payments and buyers.
- **Partners, not promises.** Licensing and customs work is filed by verified CAs and customs house agents; Globlix coordinates and stays in copy.
- **Honest numbers.** Every figure we share is based on official data. We never promise guaranteed orders or guaranteed payments.
- **Paid on results.** Under the Export Sales Partner model, our commission is earned only on export payments actually received.

## Who we serve

Manufacturers starting to export, artisans and craft clusters, and established exporters who want a second or third market.
