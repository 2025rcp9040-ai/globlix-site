International buyers search online before they ever call. We make your business easy to find and easy to trust.

## What we build

- **Export catalogue** organised by HSN code, with specifications buyers expect
- **B2B marketplace listings** and profiles
- **Buyer outreach** to importers in your target markets
- **Export Sales Partner**: we act as your export sales team for a monthly retainer plus a commission of 5–10% on export payments received

## Globlix Academy

Short practical courses for owners and teams: export basics, documentation, pricing and payments, and how to use India's free export portals.

## How it works

```steps
Catalogue | Products organised by HSN with buyer-ready specs
Listings | B2B marketplace profiles that rank
Outreach | Targeted contact with importers abroad
Enquiries to orders | We follow up and negotiate with you
```
