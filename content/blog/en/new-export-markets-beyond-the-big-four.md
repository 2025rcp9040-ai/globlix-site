Most Indian handicraft and textile exporters sell to the same four regions. Recent trade deals now make several other markets easier to enter.

```stats

99.6% | of India's exports covered by EFTA concessions
0% | on all Indian exports to New Zealand from 20 Oct 2026
```

| Market | Trade deal | Access for Indian goods | India's exports |
|---|---|---|---|
| Australia | ECTA (2022) | 0% on all tariff lines since 1 Jan 2026 | $8.5 bn (FY25) |
| Japan | CEPA (2011) | Garments enter duty-free | Garment imports $23 bn; India about 1.4% |
| Canada | CEPA talks since 2 Mar 2026 | Normal duties until a deal | $4.67 bn (FY26) |
| Latin America | Chile and Mercosur PTAs | Partial cuts | $15.17 bn (FY25) |
| EFTA | TEPA, in force 1 Oct 2025 | Concessions cover 99.6% of India's exports | $1.96 bn (FY25) |
| New Zealand | FTA, from 20 Oct 2026 | 0% on all Indian exports | $566.5 mn (FY26) |

## How to choose

Start with your HSN code. The right market is where demand for your product is large, India's share is still small, and the duty for Indian goods is at or near zero. Our free HSN snapshot does this check for you.
