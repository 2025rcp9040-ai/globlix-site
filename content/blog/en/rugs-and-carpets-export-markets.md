India is the world's third-largest exporter of carpets and floor coverings (HS 57), with **$1.81 bn** of a **$16.1 bn** world trade in 2023, behind China and Turkey. In hand-knotted rugs (HS 5701) India leads by a distance, with **38%** of world exports.

## The concentration problem

In 2023, **57%** of India's carpet exports went to the United States. In April–June 2026 that share was **60%**. When the US added a 50% tariff on Indian goods in August 2025, exporters in Bhadohi reported most US orders on hold. The rate is now normal duty plus 10% (since 24 July 2026), but the lesson is clear: one market should not decide a whole year's revenue.

## What to do now

1. **UK first.** Duty on carpets fell from up to 8% to zero for qualifying Indian rugs. Keep auditable origin records.
2. **Prepare for the EU.** Duties go only after the India–EU FTA is ratified; use the time to test and certify.
3. **Look at Saudi Arabia.** It buys $493 mn of carpets a year, and India supplies under 4%.
