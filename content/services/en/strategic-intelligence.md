Most export mistakes start with the wrong market. We use trade data at the level of your 8-digit HSN code to show where your product actually sells.

## What you get

- **Market ranking** for your HSN code: import demand, growth and India's current share
- **Duty check**: what Indian goods pay in each market, and which trade deals cut it to zero
- **Competitor view**: which countries supply each market and at what price level
- **Buyer personas**: importers, wholesalers, retailers and online marketplaces to target
- **Pricing**: landed-cost model including freight, duty and the buyer's margin

## How we research

We work from official trade statistics, government notifications and each market's published tariff schedule. Every figure in our reports can be traced to an official record, and anything that is our judgement is labelled as such.

## How it works

```steps
Your HSN code | We confirm the exact 8-digit code
Demand scan | Which countries import the most, and how fast it grows
Duty and rivals | What Indian goods pay, and who you compete with
Shortlist | Two or three markets with buyer types and target prices
```
