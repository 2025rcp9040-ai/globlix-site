This policy explains how Globlix ("we"), a brand of [legal entity name], handles personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

When you fill in a form on globlix.in we collect your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, company, location, product and export details, and your message. We also record the page you used and campaign tags in the link you followed.

## Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare the report or service you asked for, and follow up about export services. We do not sell your data.

## Where it is stored

Form entries are processed by Cloudflare (website hosting), stored in a Google Sheet and in our HubSpot CRM. These providers may store data outside India.

As long as needed to serve you, and for up to [24 months] after our last contact, unless the law requires longer.
