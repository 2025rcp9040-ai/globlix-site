India offers real money back to exporters, but many small exporters never claim it. Globlix checks your eligibility for each shipment and handles the claims.

## Main schemes (checked October 2026)

| Scheme | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| RoDTEP | Refunds embedded taxes and duties on exported goods as duty credit scrips | Extended to 31 Dec 2026 |
| RoSCTL | Refunds state and central levies on apparel and made-ups | Check current notification |
| Export Promotion Mission | Support for market access (fairs, buyer-seller meets), testing and certification, and trade finance | Active; intervention rules vary |
| Advance Authorisation | Duty-free import of inputs used in export production | Ongoing |

## How the money comes back

```steps
Ship the goods | Shipping bill filed with the right scheme declaration
Get paid | Your bank receives the export payment
eBRC issued | Proof of payment generated, needed for most claims
Claim RoDTEP | Duty credit scrip lands in your DGFT account
Use or sell the scrip | Pay import duty with it, or sell it for cash
```

## How Globlix helps

1. Check which schemes apply to your HSN codes and markets.
2. Make sure shipping bills and bank records are correct, so claims are not rejected.
3. File claims on time and, where useful, sell duty credit scrips.
4. Track renewals and rule changes for you.

Scheme rules and rates change often. We confirm the current notification before every claim.
