Getting the paperwork right is the first barrier for most new exporters. Globlix works as your single point of contact and coordinates verified chartered accountants (CAs) and customs house agents (CHAs) for each step.

## What we set up

- **Importer Exporter Code (IEC)** on the DGFT portal
- **GST for exports**: LUT filing so you export without paying IGST upfront
- **RCMC** from the right export promotion council (for example EPCH, CEPC, AEPC, TEXPROCIL)
- **AD code** registration with your bank and customs
- **Product certificates and tests** your buyer's country asks for, such as flammability tests for rugs in the US or chemical tests for the EU
- **Certificates of origin** so your buyer can claim trade-deal duty cuts (UK CETA, UAE CEPA, Australia ECTA and others)

## How it works

```steps
Share documents once | PAN, GST, bank and company papers
We prepare the files | Checklists per licence and certificate
Partners file | Verified CAs and CHAs submit on the portals
You receive certificates | IEC, LUT, RCMC, test reports, origin certificates
```

You share your documents once. We prepare the files, our partner professionals file them, and we track every step until the certificate is in your hand. Partner fees are agreed upfront.
