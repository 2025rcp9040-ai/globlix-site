Logistics is where small exporters lose margin. Globlix books and manages freight for you through vetted forwarders, so you deal with one team from factory to buyer.

## Cargo shipments

- Sea and air freight quotes from our forwarder network
- Shared containers (LCL) for small volumes
- Export customs clearance through partner CHAs
- Shipment tracking, with Globlix copied on every update

## Samples and small parcels

For samples and e-commerce parcels we set you up with courier partners' business accounts, so you get business rates from your first parcel.

You place the order with Globlix and pay one invoice; we coordinate everything else.

## How it works

```steps
Quote | Freight options from vetted forwarders
Book | One order and one invoice through Globlix
Clear customs | Partner CHA files export documents
Track | Updates until the buyer receives the goods
```
