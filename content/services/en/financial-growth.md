Export income is only real once the payment lands and the benefits are claimed. We help you keep both.

## Payments

- Choosing safer payment terms: advance, letters of credit, documents against payment
- Inward remittance set-up and **eBRC** generation so you can claim benefits
- Guidance on export credit insurance where it makes sense

## Government benefits

- **RoDTEP** claims, and sale of duty credit scrips
- **RoSCTL** for apparel and made-ups
- Support available under the **Export Promotion Mission**, including market access help for trade fairs and buyer meets
- **Advance Authorisation** for duty-free imported inputs, where relevant

Scheme rules and rates change often; we check your eligibility for each shipment before filing.

## How it works

```steps
Choose safe terms | Advance, LC or documents against payment
Receive payment | Inward remittance into your bank
Get the eBRC | Proof of export payment
Claim benefits | RoDTEP or RoSCTL, then use or sell scrips
```
